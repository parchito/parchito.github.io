Asomo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Who we are

Asomo is an Android app made by Parchito LLC ("Parchito", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy: dev@parchito.dev.

The short version

Asomo does not collect, store on our side, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive data from the app. Everything you set up in Asomo stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To work, Asomo keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your setup: which widgets and app shortcuts you added, their order and size, and your settings (panel side, size, colors and similar choices).
  • A panel picture, if you choose one: Asomo copies the picture you pick into its private storage so it can be used as the panel background.
  • A minimal crash record: if the app crashes, it notes the time and the type of error so it can avoid restarting in a loop. It contains no personal information and never leaves your device.

Uninstalling Asomo deletes all of this. You can also clear it at any time from Android Settings → Apps → Asomo → Storage → Clear storage.

Your installed apps and widgets

Asomo looks at the list of apps and widgets installed on your device so you can choose which ones to add. This happens only on your device and the list is never sent anywhere.

Widgets shown in Asomo are drawn by the apps that provide them. Asomo does not read the content of those widgets. Any data a widget shows is handled by that widget's own app under that app's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to draw the edge handle and the panel.
  • Notifications and foreground service: Android requires a notification while the edge handle is running. It is silent and contains no personal information.
  • Run at startup: to bring the edge handle back after a restart, only if you left it turned on.
  • Accessibility service (optional, off by default): used only to perform the system action you assigned to an edge gesture (for example, taking a screenshot or opening Recents) at the moment you swipe. Asomo's accessibility service does not read window content, does not observe or log what you do, and collects nothing. Asomo shows an explanation before you can turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

Purchases

Asomo Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment information. The app asks Google Play on your device whether Pro has been purchased. Google's handling of purchase data is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Children

Asomo is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of Asomo ever needs to collect data, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission in the app where required.
